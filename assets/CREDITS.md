# MOVEXA asset credits

## 3D humans (assets/human/)
Generated with MakeHuman / MPFB2 from the MakeHuman system assets (CC0 1.0, public domain). https://static.makehumancommunity.org/
MOVEXA draws its own anatomy-atlas muscle map on top of them in `coach3d.js` (muscle outlines, abs, highlighted working muscles).

## Exercise pictures (assets/steps/)
Rendered by MOVEXA from the 3D humans above with MOVEXA's own animation, muscle map and equipment (`scripts/render-steps.cjs`). Rendering uses three.js (MIT).
